Ride Log

The board records voltage, current and temperatures by itself, so your phone can stay in your pocket with the screen off. After the ride, leave the board on, connect to Owie and download the log. It is lost when the board powers off.

%RIDELOG_STATUS%

Battery learning shows whether Owie has seen enough of your pack's range to calibrate its percentage. Relearn battery forgets all of it and starts over, for example after changing the battery. It does not touch your WiFi or other settings.
